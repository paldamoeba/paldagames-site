Delete your Wine-Dark Sea data

Wine-Dark Sea, by Palda Games (Palda Services LLC).

In the game

  1. Open Fleet.
  2. Open the Leaderboard.
  3. Tap Take my ships off.

Your ships are deleted from the leaderboard right away, and the game stops sending them.

By email

Write to support@paldagames.com and ask us to delete your data. Include one of your ship names so we can find them. We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your ships on the leaderboard: their names, types, colors and game numbers, and the random player ID they were sent with.

What is not deleted

  • Your saved game stays on your phone. To remove it, erase a voyage from the game's main menu, or uninstall the game.
  • Information Google collects for ads is kept by Google under its own policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
