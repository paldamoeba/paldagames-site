Wine-Dark Sea Privacy Policy

Palda Games (Palda Services LLC). Last updated: November 8, 2026.

This policy explains what the Wine-Dark Sea game for Android collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. Wine-Dark Sea has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Saved on your phone

Your voyages, ships and settings are saved on your device. They are not sent to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Wine-Dark Sea shows rewarded video ads: you choose to watch one, for example to finish a ship sooner. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • your device's advertising ID;
  • app interactions, such as when an ad is shown or watched;
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance information.

This information is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google processes it under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings, "Ad privacy choices". Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Online leaderboard

To show the online leaderboard, the game sends a random player ID made by the game (not linked to your name or account), your ship names, ship types and colors, and your ships' game numbers (such as ships sunk and time at sea). Ship names and numbers are public on the leaderboard, so please don't use personal information as a ship name. The leaderboard runs on Cloudflare, which processes the requests to provide that service. This is used only to run the game's features.

Security

Everything the game sends over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Deleting your data

In the game, open Fleet, then Leaderboard, and tap "Take my ships off". This deletes your ships from the leaderboard and stops sending them. You can also email us and we will delete them. Full steps: Delete your data.

Children

Wine-Dark Sea is made for teens and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
